The Neighborhood

NoMa

Discover the allure of NoMa, an up-and-coming district located north of Massachusetts Avenue NE in the nation’s capital. From Union Station in the south to Q and R streets NE in the north, NoMa is a bustling hub for transportation, culture, and gastronomy.

At the heart of the neighborhood lies Union Market, a renovated mid-century food hall that is a must-visit destination for foodies and gourmands alike. Union Market offers a wide variety of culinary delights, from gourmet spices and meats to high-end kitchen supplies. Notable restaurants and shops include the Michelin-starred Italian eatery Masseria and the Dolcezza Gelato & Coffee Factory. The area surrounding the market is also home to a thriving creative scene, with businesses such as the Angelika movie theater and National Public Radio.

NoMa’s historical buildings have been revitalized and repurposed for contemporary uses. The Hecht’s Warehouse, a century-old department store storage facility, has been transformed into a distillery and a vibrant dining destination. The Uline Arena, a venue that hosted the Beatles’ first U.S. concert, has been converted into a flagship REI store, among other businesses.

As the second-busiest Amtrak hub in the country, Union Station is a major transportation hub located along Massachusetts Avenue NE. In addition to being a transit hub, Union Station offers shopping, dining, and tours. Capital Bikeshare stations are also located nearby, and the Metropolitan Branch Trail offers an eight-mile scenic route to Silver Spring, MD.

With so much to see, taste, and experience, NoMa is a must-visit destination for anyone seeking a unique and unforgettable urban adventure.
