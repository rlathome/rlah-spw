TWO-UNIT SHAWportunity! Maybe it's the touch of fresh teal trim against time-tested brick, or the Little Free Library greeting passersby…? Regardless, it's obvious that genuine whimsy infuses this welcoming Shaw row home. Step onto warmly original hardwood floors, meeting the bespoke designer tile of the renovated kitchen. The fireplace will lift your spirits through any winter frost, while the rear courtyard garden promises summertime salvation. After ascending the preciously papered and naturally lit restored central staircase, you'll find front and back bedrooms and exposed brick, along with the jewel in this queen's crown: a fully tiled spa bath with skylight, dual shower heads, and deep soaking tub, promising wet release from whatever the outside world might dish out. Speaking of dishes, however, this resplendent residence shares the block with some of the city's very best. A few doors down, next to the incomparable craft wholegrain Seylou Bakery & Mill, is the entrance to Blagden Alley. This urban wonderland serves up Michelin guide standouts Causa, The Dabney, and Tiger Fork, for starters. And desserts! Those are but a few of the delectable neighborhood denizens. At 914 N St. NW, you'll find so very much at your doorstep, and on your palate. Plus the convenience of the Convention Center Metro merely a block distant. Back home, this 2-Bed, 1.5-Bath beauty sweetens the deal with a fully separate lower-level unit, ready to serve as an elaborate guest suite or income-generating offering with proven Airbnb results. Boasting expert upgrades and pride of place location, this Shaw opportunity does not need to knock; it will seduce you with a wink.
