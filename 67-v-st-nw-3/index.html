About the Home

Description

Welcome to quaint and charming Bloomingdale and this elegant 2-bedroom, 2-bath 1,100 square foot flat in a boutique 3-unit condominium. This gorgeous light-filled home on a lovely residential tree-lined block was thoughtfully gut renovated in 2015 with many recent designer updates. You'll love the refined luxury style including custom mouldings, solid hardwood floors throughout, extra tall doors and windows with faux transoms, and a huge house-sized kitchen offering everything for the modern gourmet cook. This home has 3 private outdoor spaces, which lend the space to entertaining and hosting.

Each of the two bedrooms has its own en suite bathroom. The spacious owner's suite easily fits a king-size bed and welcomes you with a gallery wall of custom picture frame mouldings, floor-to-ceiling windows, a full glass rear door leading to the balcony (and 3rd outdoor space), a walk-in closet with built-in closet system, and a connected luxury marble bathroom featuring heated floors, a 72" double vanity, and a glass-enclosed walk-in shower.

The second bedroom fits a queen-size bed and has the second en suite full bathroom with a tub/shower combination. An interior staircase provides direct access to a massive private roof deck with Trex decking and views of the Capitol. Front-loading washer and dryer in unit.
