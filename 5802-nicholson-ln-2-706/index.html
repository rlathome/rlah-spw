About the Home

Description

Clients looking for an elusive brand new condo in Prime North Bethesda? Welcome to unit 2-706 at The Fallswood. 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom corner unit that's been renovated down to the studs, to the point where it lives and feels like brand new construction, not a resale.

Start with beautiful wide-plank flooring running throughout — with the kitchen and both baths finished in natural Gold Calacatta and Carrara marble. Every interior door, every piece of hardware, every hinge, even the balcony doors — all replaced. Designer lighting throughout, custom shades on every window, closets built out custom rather than left as builder-grade wire shelving. In 2025, two brand-new HVAC systems were installed. There's nothing left in this unit to renovate. Just move in and enjoy!
