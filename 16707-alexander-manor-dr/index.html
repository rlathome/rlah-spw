About the Home

Description

Welcome to this beautifully appointed 4-bedroom, 4.5-bath home in the rarely available Ashton Preserve community, offering spacious living areas, thoughtful updates, and exceptional spaces for both everyday living and entertaining.

The main level welcomes you with a private home office and an impressive two-story family room anchored by a striking stone fireplace. The spacious kitchen is truly the heart of the home, featuring a large center island, abundant cabinetry, and plenty of room for gathering with family and friends.

Upstairs, the generous primary suite provides a relaxing retreat with a beautifully updated, spa-like bath. A private princess suite features its own attached full bath, while two additional bedrooms share a convenient Jack-and-Jill bath—providing an ideal layout with plenty of space and privacy.

The expansive finished lower level is made for entertaining, with a large recreation area and wet bar perfect for movie nights, game days, or hosting a crowd. A versatile bonus room offers excellent space for a home gym, office, hobby room, or comfortable guest accommodations, with a full bath conveniently located nearby. An oversized storage room with a workbench provides an impressive amount of additional storage and workspace.

Even the garage stands out, featuring extensive built-in cabinetry and custom flooring for a clean, organized, finished look. Outside, enjoy a fantastic flat yard with plenty of room to play, garden, or simply relax. A peaceful patio creates the perfect setting for outdoor dining, morning coffee, or unwinding at the end of the day.

With its flexible floor plan, generous living spaces, beautiful updates, and sought-after Ashton Preserve location, this exceptional home offers the space and features today's buyers are looking for.
