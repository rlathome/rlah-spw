About the Home

Description

A new private exclusive available to show now — a strong off-market find before it reaches the broader market. Conveniently located near Glenmont Metro, this 2,500+ sq. ft. home offers the kind of space, flexibility, and convenience buyers have been searching for.

The entry level features a welcoming living area and a private office with built-ins. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms and a full bath. The lower level opens to a spacious open-concept kitchen with granite counters and stainless steel appliances, flowing seamlessly into a large addition with generous living and entertaining space, a bar, and an additional full bath. There is also another finished level that can function as additional living space, a recreation area, or even a bedroom.

Outdoor living is just as impressive, with a spacious covered deck, fenced backyard, and a large storage shed. Solar panels add even more value to this versatile home. A great opportunity to showcase something special to your clients off-market and make a strong impression through early discovery.
