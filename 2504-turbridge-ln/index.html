About the Home

Description

Welcome to 2504 Turbridge Lane, nestled in the highly sought-after Stratford Landing community of Fort Hunt. The home sits on a .33 acre lot on a cul-de-sac and has undergone a massive multi-level addition/renovation. The main living areas are anchored by standout gathering spaces. The kitchen is truly a centerpiece of the home—open, airy, and beautifully connected to a screened porch that invites seamless indoor-outdoor living. Whether you’re hosting or simply unwinding, this is a space that naturally draws you in.
Upstairs, the private quarters are well appointed across two levels. The primary suite is a true retreat and a defining highlight of the home, featuring custom built-in cabinetry and shelving, a stone-surround gas fireplace, expansive atrium windows, and two walk-in closets. The spa-inspired bath offers bluestone flooring, detailed tilework, double vanities, an oversized shower, jetted soaking tub, and exceptional storage. It’s a space that balances luxury with everyday comfort in a way that’s hard to replicate.
2 large additional bedrooms are thoughtfully positioned up an additional set of stairs for privacy and functionality - sharing a hall bath.
A finished lower level includes a full bath, laundry room with utility sink, gym space, and a large storage closet—ideal for both function and flexibility.
Outdoor living is just as impressive. A large deck, screened porch, and beautifully landscaped yard create multiple spaces to relax, entertain, and enjoy the seasons—especially in the spring when the property truly comes alive. Two sheds and a spacious two-car garage add valuable storage and practicality.
Set in Stratford Landing, the location is one of the home’s greatest strengths. Just one block from Little Hunting Creek, residents enjoy peaceful, park-like surroundings with scenic walking areas and a welcoming neighborhood feel. You’re also just a 15-minute walk to the George Washington Parkway trail, 3 miles from Mount Vernon, and moments from hiking at Fort Hunt Park. Everyday conveniences are close by at Hollin Hall Shopping Center, offering groceries, dining, services, and more—all just minutes away. With no HOA and a setting that feels both tucked away and incredibly connected, this home offers a rare opportunity to enjoy space, privacy, and lifestyle in one of Fairfax County’s most beloved communities.
