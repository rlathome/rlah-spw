About the Home

Description

Welcome to this exceptional brick-front colonial by Winchester Homes, offering over 5,000 square feet of beautifully finished living space on nearly half an acre. A grand two-story foyer makes a striking first impression, setting the tone for the scale, elegance, and thoughtful design found throughout the home.

The main level is perfectly suited for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring soaring 9-foot ceilings, a private study with custom built-ins, and a seamless open-concept layout. At the heart of the home, the gourmet kitchen impresses with a large center island, 42” cabinetry, abundant prep space, and a convenient secondary staircase to the upper level—all flowing effortlessly into the sunlit family room, highlighted by a stunning stone gas fireplace.

Upstairs, generously sized bedrooms and upper-level laundry provide everyday convenience, while the luxurious primary suite offers a true retreat with ample space to relax and unwind.

The fully finished walk-out lower level adds incredible flexibility, complete with a spacious recreation room, wet bar, bedroom, and full bath—ideal for guests, entertaining, or multi-generational living. Outdoor living is just as impressive, with a low-maintenance composite deck and a private screened porch overlooking tranquil Maryland parkland, offering peaceful wooded views and exceptional privacy.

Additional highlights include an oversized two-car garage and a large unfinished storage area. Ideally located near shopping and dining, this home is a commuter’s dream with easy access to major routes including 198, 29, 200, and I-95, providing convenient access to both Washington, DC and Baltimore.
