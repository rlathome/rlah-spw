About the Home

Description

Welcome to Chevy Chase DC and this expansive 6-bedroom, 4-bathroom detached single-family home with off-street parking. Perched atop a hill on Military Rd between Chevy Chase Parkway and Nevada Ave NW just a block and a half east of Connecticut Avenue and close to Rock Creek Park, this large residence offers a serene country estate level setting in Upper NW close to many amenities and city conveniences. The original front part of the house features a formal entry, sitting room with fireplace, and living room leading to a mudroom with side entrance, guest bedroom and adjacent updated full bathroom, and original staircase to the second level. A hallway and mudroom both lead into the kitchen and breakfast room open to the rear addition, which offers a formal dining area and massive family room. A new side craftsman style switchback stairwell lined with floor-to-ceiling windows has the new functional entrance and leads upstairs to the primary suite with en suite bathroom, laundry room, 3 more bedrooms and 3rd full bath. The lower level au pair suite is also accessed from this wing of the property and offers an office with rough in for a powder room, large owner's storage room, and 2-bedroom / 1-bath au pair or in-law suite with kitchenette, and 2nd washer & dryer. A rear driveway provides access for off-street parking for up to 3 cars. The massive nearly 14,000 sq ft lot is ready for your landscaping and gardening dreams to come to life. This over 3,600 sq ft Upper Northwest checks all the boxes for modern living with a serene and private setting in an ultra-desirable city location. About 1 Mile to Friendship Heights Metro (Red Line).
