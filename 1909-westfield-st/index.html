About the Home

Description

Set on a 0.30-acre lot beneath mature trees in the Stratford Landing neighborhood of Alexandria, 1909 Westfield Street is a classic 1961 split-foyer with a columned front portico, refreshed curb appeal, and a fully finished lower level with its own outside entrance.

The main level is arranged around an open living room, dining area, and kitchen with table space, with wood floors and a traditional layout throughout. Three bedrooms, including a primary bedroom with its own bath, sit off the hall along with a second full bath. Downstairs, a large recreation room with a fireplace and wet bar opens to the backyard through French doors, joined by two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a laundry room.

Outside, an elevated composite deck overlooks the fenced yard, with a paver patio and fire pit below. A gravel drive leads to a detached 300 sq ft outbuilding with power - perfect for a gym, home office, hobbies or extra storage. The wide front driveway provides additional off-street parking. Central air and gas forced-air heat round out the home, all within the West Potomac High School pyramid of Fairfax County Public Schools.
