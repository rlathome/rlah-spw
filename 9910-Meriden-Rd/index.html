Few opportunities present themselves to live on one of the most beautiful and coveted streets in Potomac. Welcome to 9910 Meriden, where the approach to the home is an experience in itself. Set high on the road, the residence commands a striking presence, immediately setting the tone for the elegance and scale found within. Designed with a modern sensibility, the home features expansive windows throughout that flood the interior with natural light and connect the living spaces to the surrounding landscape. A refined neutral palette enhances the home's scale and highlights its thoughtful architectural details. The sun-filled rear of the home is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring an updated kitchen and inviting family room with a gas fireplace that open to an expansive patio and resort-style pool. Set on nearly an acre, the grounds offer exceptional privacy and multiple spaces for outdoor living. The home includes five en-suite bedrooms, a beautifully designed home office with built-ins, a gracious formal dining room perfect for large gatherings and entertaining. The lower level offers a truly special experience highlighted by a showcase wine room with its own fireplace and intimate seating, evoking the atmosphere of a boutique wine lounge. This level also features a home theater, recreation space, and a bedroom with full bath. Privately set, yet remarkably convenient, the property offers easy access to nearby shopping, dining, and commuter routes, providing seamless connectivity to the city while enjoying the tranquility of one of Potomac's most prestigious settings.
