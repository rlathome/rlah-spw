About the Home

Description

Welcome to 6133 9th Road North, where thoughtful updates, flexible living space, and an unbeatable backyard come together in one of Arlington's most sought-after neighborhoods.

From the moment of arrival, this beautifully maintained single-family home welcomes with warm character and modern upgrades. The fully renovated kitchen (2020) is the heart of the main level, featuring new cabinets, appliances, countertops, and flooring, making it a true chef's space equally suited for quiet mornings and lively gatherings. Hardwood floors flow throughout the main level, adding warmth and cohesion to every room.

The main-level primary suite offers the ease and privacy of single-floor living, complete with an en suite bath featuring a separate vanity area and private toilet/shower space. A second full bathroom on this level ensures effortless everyday functionality for guests and family alike.

Outside, a deck off the main level leads to a spectacular, fully fenced backyard spanning over 11,000 square feet (just over a quarter acre). Flat, lush, and lined with mature trees along the rear, it offers natural privacy and tranquility that is truly rare for Arlington! The finished walkout lower level expands the living options significantly, offering a spacious rec room, three additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a laundry/utility room with cabinetry. It's an ideal setup for kids, guests, multigenerational living, a home office, or simply extra room to spread out.

Zoned for Cardinal Elementary School, Swanson Middle School, and Yorktown High School, Madison Manor delivers tree-lined streets, an active civic association, and a genuine sense of community. Madison Manor Park, multi-use trails, East Falls Church Metro, I-66, and the shops and restaurants of Washington and Wilson Boulevards are all minutes away. Experience inside-the-beltway living at its finest!
